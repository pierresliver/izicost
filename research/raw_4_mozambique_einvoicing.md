# Raw research 4 — Mozambique e-invoicing, fiscal devices, price data, partnership pathways (web research, 2026-09-02)

## Current state in one paragraph
Mozambique has NO real-time e-invoice/e-receipt system comparable to Brazil's NFC-e. What exists: (1) a 2012 regime requiring invoicing software to be certified by the Autoridade Tributária (AT) and to print a certification marker ("processada por computador" / "Processado por Programa Licenciado"); (2) a fiscal-machines regulation (Decreto n.º 92/2014) whose rollout stalled after a Maputo pilot in 2020–21; (3) since May 2025, a mandatory MONTHLY bulk upload of invoice data (SAF-T-style file) to the AT's e-Declaração portal, with full SAF-T MZ expected 2026. No public "verify my invoice" portal, no mandated QR/hash/ATCUD-style code on receipts, no receipt lottery. Government publishes no product/store-level prices (INE CPI aggregates only; food prices via SIMA/WFP/FEWS NET). Government is in a heavy "digital tax" push (2026 fiscal package, AI/big-data ambitions) and a World Bank-financed GovTech component exists.

## 1. Electronic invoicing
- Despacho Ministerial de 29 Fev 2012 (BR I Série n.º 9): invoicing software must be approved by AT/DGI; sequential numbering, non-editable, backups, "processada por computador". AT publishes "Lista Provisória de Softwares de Facturação (AT DGI-PMF)"; vendors cite certification numbers (e.g. Cegid Vendus n.º 2230). No mandate found for hash/QR (Zumbo Cloud's QR+hash is a vendor feature). Sources: https://www.pwc.pt/pt/pwcinforfisco/flash/mocambique/novo-procedimento-at-mocambicana-facturas-emitidas.html ; https://treeconsulting.co.mz/index.php/2025/11/10/tax-alert-procedimentos-de-comunicacao-de-facturas-a-autoridade-tributaria/ ; https://www.vendus.co.mz/blog/comunicar-faturas-autoridade-tributaria-mocambique/ ; https://www.at.gov.mz/por/Media/Files/LISTA-PROVISORIA-DE-SOFTWARES-DE-FACTURACAO-AT-DGI-PMF (despacho text itself not readable — uncertain)
- Código do IVA art. 27(10) (Lei 32/2007 as amended; Lei 22/2022 per Tree Consulting): taxpayers must submit invoice data electronically "using e-invoicing or fiscal machines".
- Aviso n.º 40/AT/DGI/2025 (27 Mar 2025): from May 2025 every VAT taxpayer (ISPC exempt) uploads a compressed XML/XLSX/CSV of the previous month's invoices to e-Declaração by month-end; high-volume issuers may submit daily/weekly. File: dates, party IDs, description of goods, unit/total values, VAT. Penalties for non-submission/uncertified software. PERIODIC reporting, not real-time clearance. Sources: https://www.ey.com/pt_mz/technical/tax-alerts/procedimento-de-comunicacao-das-facturas-emitidas-a-autoridade-tributaria-de-mocambique ; https://www.dlapiperafrica.com/en/mozambique/insights/2025/Notice-from-the-Tax-Authority-Regarding-the-Obligation-to-Submit-Invoices- ; https://www.vatupdate.com/2025/05/17/mozambique-saf-t-and-invoice-reporting/
- SAF-T MZ: developers to obtain AT specs; advisers expect full SAF-T in 2026 (no gazetted date found).
- 2026 fiscal package: Lei n.º 10/2025 de 29 Dez + Decreto n.º 52/2025, in force 1 Jan 2026; "reinforces mandatory electronic submission of invoices", terms "to be regulated" (180 days); 16% VAT on non-resident digital services with mandatory e-invoicing. Sources: https://www.rsm.global/mozambique/pt-pt/news/pacote-fiscal-2026-principais-alteracoes-fiscais-em-mocambique ; https://portaldogoverno.gov.mz/2025/12/02/conselho-de-ministros-aprecia-propostas-de-lei-que-reforcam-a-modernizacao-e-harmonizacao-do-sistema-tributario/ ; https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/02/20/mozambiques-2026-tax-reforms-digital-vat-stricter-refunds-new-thresholds-and-sector-specific-changes/ . New Código do Processo Fiscal / Aduaneiro bills approved 4 Aug 2026 (procedural): https://opais.co.mz/governo-aprova-novos-codigos-do-processo-aduaneiro-e-fiscal/
- Verify-my-invoice portal: none. e-Declaração / Portal do Contribuinte are taxpayer-login only.

## 2. Fiscal devices
Decreto n.º 92/2014 de 31 Dez (Regulamento das Máquinas Fiscais): "registadora fiscal" / "impressora fiscal" + AT back-end SGMF with published integration protocol; devices report "in real time or within the legally acceptable timeframe". Pilot in Maputo province to end-March 2021; expansion planned Q2 2021. No evidence of nationwide enforcement 2024–26; the 2025 monthly-file regime appears to have superseded it (uncertain; AT site unreachable). Sources: http://www.at.gov.mz/por/Legislacao/Legislacao-Fiscal/Maquinas-Fiscais/Maquinas-Fiscais ; https://www.at.gov.mz/por/Legislacao/Legislacao-Fiscal/Maquinas-Fiscais/Protocolo-de-integracao-entre-Maquinas-Fiscais-e-SGMF ; https://www.cciframoz.fr/pt/noticias/detalhe-da-noticia/news/factos-sobre-as-maquinas-fiscais-em-mocambique.html ; https://2ibi.com/maquinas-fiscais-transformaram-sistema-tributario-mocambicano/

- Ministério da Indústria e Comércio abolished 18 Jan 2025; internal trade and price supervision now in Ministério da Economia (Minister Basílio Muhate); Direcção Nacional do Comércio e Prestação de Serviços persists. No observatório de preços or app. https://www.presidencia.gov.mz/por/Actualidade/Extincao-e-criacao-de-Ministerios-PR-determina-redistribuicao-de-competencias

## 6. African precedents
- Rwanda EBM (mandatory 2013; real-time EIS 2021): verifiable receipt signature; EBM lottery 2015; IGC EBM data portal for government analysis only, NOT consumers. https://www.theigc.org/collections/developing-electronic-billing-machine-ebm-data-portal-rwanda
- Tanzania EFD/VFD: real-time, AT-returned QR; public verify portal https://verify.tra.go.tz/ ; lottery July 2026.
- Kenya eTIMS (all businesses Jan 2024): QR on every invoice; KRA checker + third-party consumer app "Risiti Legit". https://peopledaily.digital/business/how-kras-etims-qr-codes-can-help-kenyans-spot-fake-receipts
- Zambia Smart Invoice (2024): VSDC, QR per invoice; no consumer app.
- Angola e-factura (transition 1 Oct 2025; large taxpayers 1 Jan 2026; others 2027): QR validation code, AGT "Consultar Factura", Factura Premiada. https://www.cegid.com/ao/o-que-e-a-facturacao-electronica/
- Cabo Verde e-Fatura (DL 79/2020; real-time clearance phased 2021–22): 45-char IUD printed as QR; consumers can consult. https://efatura.cv/
- NONE has opened invoice line-item data for consumer price comparison. Brazil remains the only model.

## Assessment
(a) QR / verify-invoice replacing OCR in 1–3 years: unlikely. Chain is certified software -> monthly bulk file -> AT. No per-receipt clearance, no AT code, no verification endpoint; SGMF never scaled. 2026 law's "to be regulated" clause + SAF-T MZ could evolve toward Cabo Verde/Angola-style clearance, but nothing shows a QR mandate before 2027–28, then years of transition. Plan on OCR/vision for the whole horizon, with a parser hook for a future AT QR/IUD. Intermediate lever: certified-software vendors (Cegid Vendus/Primavera, Zumbo, 2iBi...) hold structured line-item data and could be API partners for retailer opt-in feeds.
(b) Proposing to government: plausible as a "demand-side compliance" pitch, not as a data-source request. AT will not share invoice data (no legal basis, weak data-protection framework per 2025 diagnostic) but wants citizens demanding receipts and digital compliance. Frame IziCost as receipt-demand nudging + anonymised informal-market price intelligence, in exchange for endorsement, certified-software list access, and a seat on any future QR spec. Doors: 1) AT — DGI / Projecto Máquinas Fiscais (DGI-PMF), Conselho da Fiscalidade; Linha do Contribuinte 1266, linhadocontribuinte@at.gov.mz. 2) MCTD/INAGE via EDGE GovTech component (US$30m, closes Feb 2027). 3) Ministério da Economia — Direcção Nacional do Comércio e Prestação de Serviços (cesta básica dashboard). 4) INE Price Department; WFP RAM / SIMA. 5) TEDI successor / ATAF / World Bank PFM advisers as introducers; ProConsumers, OMR as civil-society validators.
Uncertainties: exact text of 2012 despacho and Decreto 92/2014; gazetted SAF-T MZ date; current EDGE implementing ministry; any regulation of the 2026 e-invoicing clause published since June 2026.
